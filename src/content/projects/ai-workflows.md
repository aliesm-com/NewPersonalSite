## AI as part of the product

At Ai Run, I develop backends for AI-driven applications and integrate language models and NLP into working products. The focus is on connecting useful model capabilities to the services, interfaces, and workflows around them.

## From prototype to delivery

Python, Django, and Go provide the application foundation. Streamlit and n8n support interfaces and automation, while Docker and CI/CD support repeatable deployment and operation.
