## Systems that connect the physical and digital

My work at Neurocage brings together embedded software, connected devices, data processing, and the tools teams use to operate them. Reliability, performance, and production stability are central to that work.

## From devices to useful insights

I build software that integrates with hardware, and AI-assisted workflows that process data, produce insights, and visualize results. Django management panels connect those capabilities to daily operations.

## Engineering across boundaries

The role also includes delivery pipelines and architecture reviews across teams. It requires following problems through the whole system, from device behavior to backend integration and deployment.
