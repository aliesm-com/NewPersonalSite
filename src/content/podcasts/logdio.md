## Following the questions

Logdio explores mysteries, unresolved cases, and strange events. The story begins with what happened, then follows the evidence, competing explanations, and questions that remain.

## Curiosity, with a critical eye

The approach combines narrative with analysis: looking at scientific explanations, the limits of available information, and the human side of a case. Uncertainty is part of the story.
