## The stories behind the code

Bugdio is about the experiences that come with being a programmer: the bug that takes an unexpected turn, the hard lesson, the satisfying fix, and the ordinary moments that become memorable stories.

## Sharing the experience

The focus is on the people doing the work and what they learn along the way. Technical problems provide the starting point; lived experience gives the stories their shape.
